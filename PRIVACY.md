# NVRBEATS Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026  
**Publisher:** SL-STUDIO  
**Privacy contact:** SL_Studios@hotmail.com

SL-STUDIO (“we,” “us,” or “our”) publishes NVRBEATS, a Windows desktop application for recording and managing computer playback audio. This policy explains what information NVRBEATS processes, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

## Information NVRBEATS processes

### Audio recordings and recording details

When you choose to record, NVRBEATS captures audio playing through your selected Windows playback output using WASAPI loopback. It does not use your microphone. The recording is saved to the folder you choose on your computer.

The app also processes recording details you enter or the app creates, including file name and path, set name, artist, date, duration, format, file size, genre, location, notes, tags, and artwork path. Your recording preferences are stored locally.

Audio, recording details, and preferences are stored on your device. NVRBEATS does not upload recordings to SL-STUDIO. If you choose to share a recording or other information with another person or service, that recipient’s privacy policy applies to its use of the information.

### Optional account and connected-tool sign-in

The core recorder does not require an account. If you choose to use the optional sign-in or connected assistant/tool features, authentication services process information needed to create and manage your account. This may include your email address, account identifier, password credentials, and authentication/session tokens. Email sign-in is handled through Supabase; Google sign-in uses Google OAuth through the configured Lovable authentication integration and Supabase.

We use this information to authenticate you and support the sign-in or connected-tool feature you chose. The authentication providers process information under their own privacy policies and service terms. If you connect an assistant or other third-party tool, it may receive information you choose to authorize or send to it; that service’s privacy policy applies to its handling of the information.

## How information is used

We use local information to record, play, organize, and manage audio files and app preferences on your device. Optional account information is used to authenticate you and support connected-tool features. NVRBEATS does not use recordings for advertising or profiling and does not include an analytics service in the current build.

## Storage, sharing, and security

Audio recordings and their details remain in the folders and local app storage on your computer. They are subject to the security of your Windows account and device. The app does not separately encrypt recording files.

For optional sign-in, account information is processed by the authentication services described above. Those providers may process information in other jurisdictions under their own policies. SL-STUDIO does not sell personal information.

NVRBEATS does not automatically send recordings to SL-STUDIO. Information is shared with a connected service only when you choose to use that service and authorize or provide the relevant information. Microsoft separately processes information associated with Microsoft Store downloads, updates, and your Microsoft account under Microsoft's privacy terms.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device and Windows account protected, and back up recordings you want to preserve.

## Retention and deletion

Recordings remain on your device until you delete them. You can delete recordings in NVRBEATS or through Windows File Explorer. Recording details and preferences remain in local app storage until you remove them or clear that storage. Uninstalling NVRBEATS may not delete recordings stored in folders you selected.

Optional account information is retained by the authentication services while needed to provide the account and is subject to their retention practices. To request access to or deletion of account information associated with NVRBEATS, contact SL-STUDIO at the address below.

## Your choices and privacy rights

You can use the core recorder without signing in. You choose when to start and stop recording, where recordings are saved, and whether to use optional sign-in or connected services. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Contact us to make a privacy request.

## Children

NVRBEATS is a general-purpose audio utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when NVRBEATS or its data practices change. The current version and effective date will be available wherever this policy is published.
